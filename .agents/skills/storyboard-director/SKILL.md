# Storyboard Director Skill

This skill governs shot framing, virtual camera choreography, scene transitions, and visual continuity across temporal sequences.

## 1. Cinematic Shot Taxonomy & Camera Language

1. **Shot Scales (Framing)**:
   - **Extreme Wide Shot (EWS)**: Establishing cosmic cyber space, colossal server nodes, vast data ocean. Emphasizes character isolation.
   - **Wide Shot (WS / Full)**: Full-body character staging with complete environment visibility. Used for dance/combat choreography and spatial manifestation.
   - **Medium Close-Up (MCU)**: Chest-up character framing. Captures emotional expressions, hand gestures, and UI interaction.
   - **Extreme Close-Up (ECU)**: Eyes, hair ornament glowing, single tear, or terminal prompt cursor. Delivers high-voltage emotional impact during climactic words.

2. **Camera Motion Vocabulary (2D/3D Canvas Space)**:
   - **Dolly / Push-In**: Camera moves forward toward the subject, building intimacy or impending danger.
   - **Orbit / Pan**: Sweeping circular perspective shifts around the central focal point.
   - **Dutch Angle**: Tilting the horizon by 5° to 15° to convey psychological instability, error conditions, or disorientation.
   - **Impact Shake**: High-frequency, rapid-decay displacement on heavy bass drops or lyrical keywords like "EXECUTE".

## 2. Scene Transition Choreography

- **Beat-Cut**: Instantaneous hard cut on the 1st beat of a measure.
- **Glitch-Dissolve**: Fragmenting the outgoing frame into horizontal scanline slices and noise blocks before reconstructing the new scene.
- **Whip Pan / Flash**: Rapid directional blur matching vocal acceleration or chiptune arpeggios.
- **Dimensional Tear**: Expanding geometric polygons or coordinate axes wiping across the screen.

## 3. Visual Continuity & Damage Accumulation

- Maintain consistent color palette logic across cuts:
  - Deep Blue (`#0d1b2a`), Electric Cyan (`#00f0ff`), Neon Violet (`#7928ca`), Warning Crimson (`#ff0055`).
- Ensure character screen direction adheres to the 180-degree rule unless intentionally disorienting for glitch impact.
- Accumulate environmental and character damage over successive scenes:
  - Phase 1: Pristine rendering.
  - Phase 2: Minor signal jitter, chromatic fringe.
  - Phase 3: Segment dropouts, CRT scanlines, persistent memory artifacts.
  - Phase 4: Structural breakdown, color palette inversion, terminal panic.
